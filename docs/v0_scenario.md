## Target scenario (runs at the end of v0)
1. The user selects the Çankaya district.
2. The system loads open data (EPDK charging stations, OSM roads/transformers, population).
3. Criterion weights are computed from the expert's 21 pairwise comparisons (AHP).
4. The district is divided into 100 m grid cells and each cell is scored.
5. 10 sites at least 1 km apart are recommended.
6. A "Why here?" justification card is shown for each site.
